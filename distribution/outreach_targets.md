# What NOT to do

- ❌ Don't blast cold emails — DMs only
- ❌ Don't pitch features — pitch the take rate (0.5% vs 5–20% is the only line that matters)
- ❌ Don't ask for stars/follows — ask for ONE mission posted
- ❌ Don't over-explain crypto — assume they get it (these are crypto people)
- ❌ Don't mention SafeAgent / token safety as the primary thing — lead with bounty marketplace
- ❌ Don't share Telegram/private numbers in cold DMs

# What to do

- ✅ Lead with the take rate
- ✅ Show one specific mission they could post (concrete > abstract)
- ✅ Offer to seed with $20–50 USDC yourself for the first one
- ✅ Ask for ONE thing: post a mission OR give feedback on the spec
- ✅ Include `cryptogenesis.duckdns.org/missions` in every DM
- ✅ Track DM responses in a spreadsheet (or just count)
