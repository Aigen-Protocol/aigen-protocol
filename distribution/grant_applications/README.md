# STELLA Grant Applications — Submission queue

Three applications drafted, ready to submit. Submit in this order — they're
independent and a Yes from any one unblocks audit funding.

## Order of submission

### 1. Aave Grants DAO (best fit pre-deployment) — submit first

**URL:** https://aavegrants.org/

Aave just received $25M for stablecoin work in Apr 2026. Most aligned funder
for a stablecoin reference implementation. Pre-deployment is OK (they fund
pre-deploy). Audit-funding precedent.

**Application:** [`01_aave_grants_dao.md`](./01_aave_grants_dao.md)

**Realistic probability:** 20-35%. They're funding a lot of stablecoin work
right now. Our pitch is unique (anti-Terra reference). $30-50k is in their
typical range.

**Process:** Form on aavegrants.org. Aave Grants DAO meets weekly. Decisions
typically in 2-4 weeks.

### 2. Uniswap Foundation Security Track — submit second

**URL:** https://www.uniswapfoundation.org/grants

UF committed $24M to security in 2025. Audit-funding is exactly their
mandate. Our angle: STELLA is security research disguised as a stablecoin —
the artifact is the audited reference design.

**Application:** [`02_uniswap_foundation_security.md`](./02_uniswap_foundation_security.md)

**Realistic probability:** 15-25%. UF is more selective. Our story is
strong but they typically fund more established teams.

**Process:** Form on uniswapfoundation.org. Cycles run every ~6 weeks.

### 3. Base Batches 2026 — submit third (cohort program)

**URL:** https://www.basebatches.xyz/

8-week virtual cohort. $10k grant + $50k investment for top 3. Pre-deployment
explicitly allowed (it's an early-stage program).

**Application:** [`03_base_batches_2026.md`](./03_base_batches_2026.md)

**Realistic probability:** 10-25%. Cohort selection is competitive but our
pre-existing build velocity (AIGEN already shipped, 9 daemons running) is a
strong signal.

**Process:** Application form. Cohorts kick off quarterly.

## What to do after submitting all 3

1. **Don't wait passively.** Continue building STELLA testnet deployment in
   parallel. Even without grants, testnet deploy is $0. Showing momentum
   strengthens any application.

2. **Public-build in the open.** Tweet/Farcaster the testnet deployment.
   Each grant program is influenced by public traction.

3. **If 0 grants approved by week 6:** Evaluate the alternative paths:
   - Apply to Gitcoin Grants (quadratic funding, individual donors)
   - Reach out to specific stablecoin teams (Frax, MakerDAO, Liquity) for
     potential collaboration / co-funding
   - Consider scoping STELLA as testnet-only public reference, no mainnet
   - Or self-fund via AIGEN protocol fees (slow path; would take ~12+ months)

4. **If grant approved:** Book the audit immediately. Don't sit on the funding.

## Backup programs if all 3 reject

- **Gitcoin Grants** (quadratic funding) — https://www.gitcoin.co/grants — open
  any time, smaller amounts, requires community matching
- **Optimism Onchain Builders** mission — https://atlas.optimism.io/missions/retro-funding-onchain-builders — requires deployment first; apply post-testnet
- **MakerDAO Grants** (Sky) — funds DAI ecosystem work; STELLA could pitch as
  complementary low-supply alternative
- **Frax Grants** — frx ecosystem, smaller pool but stablecoin-focused
